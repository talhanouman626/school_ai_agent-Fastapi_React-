# Admissions
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/admissions

## Key Dates
- Admission cycle begins in February for session starting August
- Academic year: August to July

## Admission Open For
- Playgroup to Class 9 (new academic year)
- Mid-term transfers: limited seats, subject to entrance test

## Admission Criteria
Three things are checked:
1. Age Range
2. Test and Interview Clearance
3. Seat Availability

Note: Registration does not guarantee admission.

## Hifz Admission Criteria
- Age: 7 to 8 years
- Strong Academic Record required
- Must clear MGS Hifz Admission Test
- Successful Interview with both parents and student

## Admission Process (5 Steps)
1. Fill out Registration Form at mgs.edu.pk/registration
2. Pay Registration Fee (within two days)
3. Receive Outline for Admission Test (mgs.edu.pk/admissions/course-outline.html)
4. Clear Admission Test
5. Clear Interview

## Required Documents
1. Student's Birth Certificate / B-Form (NADRA)
2. Copies of Parents' CNIC (both parents or guardian)
3. Academic Reports and School Leaving Certificate from previous school
4. Three passport size coloured photographs
5. Guardianship Certificate (if applicable)

### Available Discounts
- Siblings Discount
- Izmir Residents Discount (Izmir Campus applications only)
- Employee Discount

Note: Only one discount can be availed at a time. All concessions valid for one academic year only.

## FAQs
- **Is there a sibling discount?** Yes, available. Only one discount at a time.
- **Is there an entrance test for Nursery?** No, only informal interaction/interview.
- **When does the academic year start?** August, admission cycle starts February.
