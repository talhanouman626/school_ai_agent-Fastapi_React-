# Admissions
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/admissions

## Admission Open For
- Nursery to Class 9 (new academic year)
- Limited seats available for mid-term transfers (subject to clearance of entrance test)

## Age Requirements
- **Nursery:** 3.5 to 4.5 years
- **Prep/KG:** 4.5 to 5.5 years
- **Class 1:** 5.5 to 6.5 years

## Required Documents
1. Child's Birth Certificate (original + copy)
2. Parent's CNIC copy
3. Previous school Result Card (if applicable)
4. 4 passport size photos of child
5. 2 passport size photos of each parent

## Admission Process
MGS School ka admission process nihayat transparent hai. Parents ko chahiye ke wo office hours ke dauran campus visit karein taake wo school environment ko dekh sakein aur admission form collect karein.

* **Step 1:** Visit school office to collect admission form.
* **Step 2:** Submit filled form with documents.
* **Step 3:** Child's written test (Class 3 and above) for English, Math, and Science.
* **Step 4:** Interview with Principal.
* **Step 5:** Fee deposit within 3 days of selection.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- **Do you offer sibling discounts during admission?** Yes, 10% discount on monthly fee for second child.
- **Is there an entrance test for Nursery?** No, only an informal interaction/interview with the child and parents.
