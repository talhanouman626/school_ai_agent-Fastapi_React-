- Days: Tuesday to Thursday only
- Time: 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM
- Appointment required in advance
- Original CNIC must be brought — no entry without it

- Any change in address, phone etc. must be reported immediately
- Submit written application to Communication Department
- OR request via MGS Parents App

### Student Affairs
- Only parents or legal guardians can discuss child's progress
- Relatives and private tutors not entertained

## School Rules and Regulations

### Late Arrival Policy
- Reporting time: 07:45 AM
- Gates close at: 07:50 AM
- Students arriving after 07:50 AM will be sent home

### Early Departure Policy
- Students cannot leave early except for emergency or illness
- For planned early departure: notify school office 1 day before
- OR submit written application at Gate No. 2 by 08:00 AM
- Unwell students should not come to school

### Uniform and Hygiene Policy
- Playgroup: No uniform required
- Nursery onwards: Uniform mandatory
- Girls Grade I onwards: Scarf required as part of uniform
- Girls Grade VI onwards: Abaya mandatory
- Uniform must be neat and clean
- Hair: properly combed, trimmed, free of lice
- Nails: neat and properly trimmed
- Personal hygiene: regular showers, handwashing, grooming, dental care

### Student ID Card Policy
- ID Card required to leave school premises
- Parents must show ID Card when picking up child
- Forgotten ID Card: gate pass available from Communication Dept (Rs. 100 from 3rd time)
- Lost ID Card: Rs. 500 fee for reissuance
- Damaged ID Card: free replacement (show to coordinator first)
