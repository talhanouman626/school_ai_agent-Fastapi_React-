# Professional Development
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/professional-development.html

## Overview
MGS believes academic excellence cannot be achieved without investing in staff. Professional development is regularly conducted through workshops, seminars, and collaborative sessions to cultivate continuous learning among educators.

## Goals
- Equip teachers with latest educational methodologies
- Technology integration in teaching
- Effective classroom management strategies

## Training Partner Institutions
MGS has successfully conducted training programmes in collaboration with:
1. University of Cambridge
2. Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)
3. Ali Institute of Education
4. Teachers' Development Centre
5. Institute of Integrated Knowledge
6. Naseeha Institute

## Additional Programmes
- Self-paced and online courses for teachers and management staff
- Targeted professional trainings by national and international trainers
- Tarbiyah programmes conducted by renowned motivational speakers
