To foster innovation at MGS, we believe that academic excellence cannot be achieved without investing in our staff. Therefore, professional development is essential for meeting the evolving needs of our students. To enhance the skills and knowledge of our educators, we regularly conduct professional development activities such as workshops, seminars, and collaborative sessions. These initiatives aim to cultivate a culture of continuous learning, empowering our teachers with the latest educational methodologies, technology integration, and effective classroom management strategies.  
  
**While we actively seek beneficial collaborators, we have successfully conducted training programmes in collaboration with various institutions, including;**

  * University of Cambridge
  * Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)
  * Ali Institute of Education
  * Teachers’ Development Centre
  * Institute of Integrated Knowledge
  * Naseeha Institute



Additionally, we offer self-paced and online courses for both teachers and management staff. Furthermore, we regularly organize targeted professional trainings and tarbiyah programmes conducted by renowned national and international trainers and motivational speakers.
