# Life at MGS
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/life-at-mgs.html

## Academics
Academic activities at MGS are deeply rooted in tarbiyah. From preschool onward, children learn through the lens of Islam. This blend of Islamic values with contemporary education distinguishes MGS from other institutions.

## Extra and Co-curricular Activities
Students participate in sports competitions, calligraphy exhibitions, clubs, and community service. Key events include Retreat Nights, Seerah Week, and various engagement programmes that build well-rounded, practicing Muslim personalities.

### MUNs and Debates
- Students participate in Model United Nations (MUN) and debate competitions
- Develops critical thinking, research, and public speaking skills
- Training sessions, mentorship, and practice rounds provided
- Students compete in national and international MUN conferences

### Seerah Week
- Annual event exploring life and teachings of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)
- Activities: storytelling, discussions, competitions, creative projects
- Guest speakers, multimedia presentations, reflective exercises
- Builds students' connection with Islamic faith and principles

### Retreat Nights
- Overnight spiritual gatherings for students
- Activities: motivational talks, Quranic recitations, guided meditations, teamwork
- Strengthens emotional and spiritual well-being
- Builds community and Islamic identity

### Educational Trips
- Visits to historical landmarks, science museums, cultural institutions
- Recreational outings: adventure parks, outdoor explorations, heritage tours
- Complements classroom learning with hands-on experiences

### Explore Camp
- Outdoor camp developing resilience, leadership, survival skills
- Activities: teamwork, self-reliance, environmental responsibility
- Expert supervision with educational and character-building challenges

### Events and Workshops
- Senior School Council (Grade 8 and above)
- Teacher Assistant Elections (Junior School)
- Leadership Camps
- Academic competitions: Kangaroo Science Competition, Mathelon, Spelling Bee
- School events: Farewell Ceremony, Bonfire Night, Meena Bazar
