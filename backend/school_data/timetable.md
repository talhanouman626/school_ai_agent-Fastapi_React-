# School Timetable
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/timetable
last_updated: 2025-05-14

## Important Notice
Bari meherbani dhiyaan dein ke mosam ki tabdeeli ya hukumat ke notification ki wajah se school timings mein fori tabdeeli ki ja sakti hai. Parents ko SMS aur WhatsApp ke zariye itla di jayegi.

## Regular Schedule (Mon - Fri)
| Period | Time Slot | Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arrival** | 07:45 AM | Student Entry |
| **Assembly** | 08:00 AM - 08:15 AM | Morning Prayer |
| **Period 1** | 08:15 AM - 09:00 AM | Core Subjects |
| **Break** | 09:45 AM - 10:15 AM | Refreshments |
| **Lunch Break** | 11:45 AM - 12:15 PM | Prayer & Lunch |
| **Departure** | 01:45 PM | Home Time |

## Weather / Emergency Schedule
- **Foggy Days:** School starts at 09:30 AM.
- **Early Closure:** During extreme heatwaves, school may close at 12:00 PM.
- **Saturdays:** School remains closed for students.
