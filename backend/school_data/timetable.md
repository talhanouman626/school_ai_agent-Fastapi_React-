# School Timetable
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/timetable

## Regular Schedule (Monday to Friday)
| Period | Time | Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Arrival | 07:45 AM | Student Entry |
| Assembly | 08:00 AM - 08:15 AM | Morning Prayer |
| Period 1 | 08:15 AM - 09:00 AM | Core Subjects |
| Break | 09:45 AM - 10:15 AM | Refreshments |
| Lunch Break | 11:45 AM - 12:15 PM | Prayer and Lunch |
| Departure | 01:45 PM | Home Time |

## Special Schedules
- Foggy Days: School starts at 09:30 AM
- Extreme Heatwave: School may close at 12:00 PM
- Saturdays: School closed for students

## Important Notice
Timings may change due to weather or government notification. Parents will be informed via SMS and WhatsApp.
