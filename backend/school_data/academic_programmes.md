### Preschool

  * Play Group
  * Nursery
  * Kindergarten



The Preschool programme at MGS prioritizes the integration of Islamic values alongside the UK Early Years Foundation Stage curriculum. We promote experiential learning to create a stimulating environment for young minds, fostering curiosity and interest in learning. Additionally, we offer numerous tarbiyah programmes for young children to start building a connection with Allah SWT early on, helping them perceive the world through a mindful lens.

‹›

### Junior School

  * Grade I
  * Grade II
  * Grade III
  * Grade IV
  * Grade V



Our Junior School programme integrates the Cambridge Curriculum with the Single National Curriculum (SNC) to provide a comprehensive educational experience. Our primary aim is to foster academic excellence while also nurturing essential soft skills. Through this approach, we empower students to become autonomous learners and critical thinkers, preparing them for success beyond the classroom. Additionally, our tarbiyah-driven extracurricular activities and afterschool clubs serve to enhance skill development and spiritual enrichment, complementing our academic curriculum.

### Middle School

  * Grade VI
  * Grade VII



MGS Middle School programme is based on a Cambridge accredited curriculum which provides a pathway for both O Level and Matric. We strive to nurture our students' growing potential by providing them with a supportive environment. Additionally, we enhance their school experience by offering tarbiyah and engagement opportunities beyond academics, which are designed to align with their maturity level and age-specific needs.

‹›

### O Level

  * Grade VIII O
  * Grade IX O
  * Grade X O
  * Grade XI O



Our O Level programme is affiliated with the University of Cambridge, UK for the CAIE examination as a British Council Partner School. With a team of qualified faculty members, we prioritize practical application, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills alongside textbook knowledge. Moreover, we provide various avenues for engagement and personal development outside the classroom, ensuring students have the opportunity to explore their interests and talents. Despite being enrolled in external programmes, we ensure that students are not deprived of tarbiyah and the reinforcement of our values throughout their academic journey.

‹›

### Matriculation

  * Grade VIII M
  * Grade IX M
  * Grade X M



Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE), Lahore, our Matriculation programme offers more than textbook learning. We create an environment that challenges our students, with qualified faculty members providing guidance and support. Additionally, ample opportunities are available for students to explore themselves outside the classroom, fostering holistic development and personal growth. While enrolled in external programmes, students receive tarbiyah in a manner that fosters their personal development.

‹›

### Hifz Programme

The three-year optional Hifz programme at MGS emphasizes Quran memorization within a supportive and encouraging environment alongside basic education. This ensures that students can catch up easily after completing Hifz. The programme is exclusively available for boys and upon completion, students are equipped to appear in the Wifaq-ul-Madaris Al-Arabia, Pakistan examination.
