# Academic Programmes
source_url: https://www.mgs.edu.pk/academic-programmes.html

## Programmes Overview
MGS offers 6 academic programmes from Playgroup to O Level and Matric.

| Programme | Grades | Curriculum |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Preschool | Playgroup, Nursery, Kindergarten | UK Early Years Foundation Stage |
| Junior School | Grade I to V | Cambridge + Single National Curriculum (SNC) |
| Middle School | Grade VI to VII | Cambridge Accredited (pathway to O Level & Matric) |
| O Level | Grade VIII O to XI O | University of Cambridge CAIE (British Council Partner) |
| Matriculation | Grade VIII M to X M | BISE Lahore affiliated |
| Hifz Programme | All levels (Boys only) | Quran memorization + basic education |

---

## Preschool (Playgroup, Nursery, Kindergarten)
- Curriculum: UK Early Years Foundation Stage
- Focus: Experiential learning, Islamic values, tarbiyah
- Children build connection with Allah SWT from early age

## Junior School (Grade I to V)
- Curriculum: Cambridge + SNC combined
- Focus: Academic excellence + soft skills
- Tarbiyah-driven extracurricular and afterschool clubs

## Middle School (Grade VI to VII)
- Curriculum: Cambridge accredited
- Pathway to both O Level and Matric
- Tarbiyah and engagement opportunities beyond academics

## O Level (Grade VIII O to XI O)
- Affiliated with University of Cambridge, UK (CAIE)
- MGS is a British Council Partner School
- Focus: Critical thinking, practical application, problem-solving
- Tarbiyah continues throughout O Level journey

## Matriculation (Grade VIII M to X M)
- Affiliated with BISE (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education), Lahore
- Focus: Holistic development + tarbiyah
- Qualified faculty with personal guidance and support

## Hifz Programme
- Duration: 3 years
- Available for: Boys only (all grade levels)
- Focus: Quran memorization in supportive environment + basic education
- After completion: Students appear in Wifaq-ul-Madaris Al-Arabia, Pakistan exam
- Students can easily catch up academically after completing Hifz
